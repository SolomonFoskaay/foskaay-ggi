The room your players never pay to enter

Foskaay GGI (Foskaay Gasless Games Infrastructure) is an open rail for Arc. You delegate your game and player contracts to a session, everything inside runs for free through your own rules, and one settlement closes it. Players never pay gas. You pay a fraction of a cent per match.

Why this exists

On Solana, an ephemeral rollup can take your game accounts off the base chain, run everything for free inside, and commit the result back. That is a huge unlock for games, because the player never sees gas. Arc is an EVM chain with USDC as its gas token, and it has no such rollup. So I built the same effect in a simpler way, and named it the Foskaay GGI Midchain.

Inside a session, every move, dice roll, timer and point credit is a signed message run through your own pure rules, off the base chain. Nothing you do inside costs anything. Only two moments are real Arc transactions: the connect and the settle. That is why a 200-move game costs the same as a 5-move game, and why the player never sees a wallet popup or a gas top-up.

Provable randomness is part of the core, at no extra cost. Your game commits a seed at connect, every roll, card draw or lootbox is derived from that seed through your own rules, and the seed is revealed at settle. Any player can replay it in the Explorer and check it, so dice, cards and lootboxes are fair by default. It is live on Arc mainnet now.

The shape I recommend

Two accounts, however many games you run:

  • One game contract. It holds the rules and the match. Every game you add is a module or a bucket inside it, not a new account.
  • One player account. Points, lives and records for every game, bucketed by game tag. It is delegated with the match, so crediting a point is a free write inside the room.

That is two delegated accounts for any number of games. It does not grow when you add game number 50. The alternative, a separate account per game or per feature, is allowed, but every extra account is a separate delegation and a separate cost, so I recommend the two-account shape in the docs.

Who it is for

  • Game developers on Arc who want their players to never touch gas.
  • Teams porting a game that is already on-chain and wants a cheaper, gasless loop.
  • Builders with a contract that needs reshaping to get the most out of the Midchain, which is where hire me helps.
  • Verifiers and reviewers who want to check real on-chain usage on Arc in the Explorer.

Open by design

The contracts are upgradeable and permanent, the core is open, and the docs are public. You can integrate it yourself for free, or ask me to do it with you. Either way, your game owns its economy and the rail only owns the room.
