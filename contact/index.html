What to include

To get a useful first reply, tell me a little:

  • Your game. A link, or one paragraph on what it is and where it runs today (on-chain or not).
  • Your chain and stack. Arc, EVM, Solidity plus the framework you use.
  • What you want. Gasless play, cheaper sessions, a port, or a full build. The more specific, the faster I can help.
  • Where you are. Testnet or mainnet, and roughly when you want to ship.
If you already know you want hands-on help, the Hire me page has the services and how I work. Otherwise, the docs quickstart is the fastest way to try the rail yourself.
