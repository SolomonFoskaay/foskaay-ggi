Ludo, fully on-chain

A Ludo match on Foskaay Gasless Games Infrastructure. The contract owns the rules, the dice, the crown and the points. During play every move runs free inside the Foskaay GGI Midchain, and only the connect and the settle are transactions. The sponsor pays those two, never you. This page only displays what the chain says.

Open the Foskaay GGI Explorer to verify the session, the seed and the midchain moves.

Press New match to connect a session and start. You are seat 0.
