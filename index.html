FOR GAME DEVELOPERS ON ARC

Make your on-chain game gasless for players, and cheap for you

Foskaay Gasless Games Infrastructure (Foskaay GGI) is the rail that empowers game devs to build fully on-chain gasless games with a web2-like flow without annoying wallet pop-ups mid-game or players paying for game-plays on the Arc Blockchain, similar to what ephemeral rollups like MagicBlock ER does on the Solana Blockchain. You delegate your game and player contracts to a session, everything inside runs free through your own rules including free access to provable randomness for dice, lootboxes and more, and settlement closes it. Players never pay gas and never see a wallet popup. You pay a fraction of a cent per match, in USDC.

The whole idea in one line

Delegate your game contracts to a session, let the players play for free inside the Foskaay GGI Midchain, then settle once. Your game stays the source of truth; the rail is only the room.

// 1. connect one session and delegate your game + player contracts await ggi.handoverWithAccounts({ sessionId, gameLogic: myGame, players: [p0, p1], sessionKeys: [k0, k1], accounts: [myGame, myPlayers], games: 1, }); // 2. play for free. every move runs your own rules, off the base chain // 3. settle once. the result, the board and the points land on-chain await ggi.settle({ sessionId, finalHash, seedReveal, sigs, signers });

No fork. No contract to copy. You keep your economy; the rail owns the room. If you would rather I do it with you, see Hire me.

How it works

  • Delegate. You delegate your game contract and your player account into one Foskaay GGI session. That is the free room.
  • Play for free. Every move, dice roll, timer and point credit runs inside the Foskaay GGI Midchain through your own pure rules. No per-action transaction, for the player or for you.
  • Settle once. One transaction commits the match, the board and every point to Arc. The player account is credited in the same step, so points persist forever.
  • Verify. The connect and the settle are real Arc events. Anyone can replay the session in the Explorer and check the signatures against the on-chain result.

Why developers pick it

  • Your players never hit the gas wall. No top-ups, no failed transactions on a phone. That is the single biggest drop-off in web3 games, gone.
  • You pay per match, not per move. A 200-move game costs the same as a 5-move game. Heavy games are not punished.
  • Stablecoin gas. Arc uses USDC as gas, so your costs are predictable, not tied to a volatile token.
  • Nothing to run. No validator, no rollup node, no RPC to babysit. A sponsor key pays the two transactions.
  • Fully on-chain. Your contract is the source of truth. The browser only displays what the chain returns.
  • Verifiable. Real usage, real transactions, a public Explorer and measured costs, all on Arc.
  • Provable randomness, free. Dice, cards and lootboxes derive from the committed seed through the core's free pure random(); nobody pays for it, and anyone can verify it at settle.
