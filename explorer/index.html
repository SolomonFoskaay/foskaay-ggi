Midchain moves (gasless, off-base, verifiable)

Inside a session, every move runs through the PURE game contract for free and is signed by the session key into a hash chain. Paste a session id to load its published move log and check the chain ends at the on-chain settled final hash.

The rules are pure functions: the board and the points are compact bytes the contract returns (no images on-chain), so the whole match runs with free calls. The game contract holds the match and, at settle, writes the games and credits the player account in one step. Only the connect and the settle are transactions.

Honest limits

Inside a session, moves run off the base chain: they are signed by the session key and tied together by a hash chain, so a thousand moves cost the same as one. That is the design that makes play free, so there are no per-move transactions to list. The on-chain proof of a session is the connect event (Handover), the settled result (Settled), the revealed seed and the fee. A game that wants per-move visibility can publish its signed move log, and this explorer can render it; without that, per-move detail stays off-chain by design.
Settlement is submitted by a relayer, which is trusted for timing, never for truth. The signatures decide what is true, and the contracts enforce it.
