Earn Competition (AGM)

AGM means Automated Game Matcher: a first-of-its-kind idea for games, invented by the founder of GlobalFolkGames, Solomon Foskaay. It borrows the best of the trading world (CEX and DEX) and turns a gameplay match into something the players can open, take, and settle, just like a token or an NFT. The point is simple: native gamers show their prowess against players worldwide, and the winner of each match earns a share of the competition pot. Every step is recorded on a chain you can check.

How it works (the plain version)

  1. Post a match: pick your game and the prize level. This writes your open match on-chain, so it is public and provable.
  2. Take a match: another player (or the computer backer) accepts your open match. You can never accept your own match.
  3. Play: two players, one winner. The prize pool is locked the moment the match is taken.
  4. Settle: the winner's share is finalized on-chain and ready to be paid out.

Post a match

Matches run in one coin only: USDC. The amount you see is the amount you post and the amount the winner receives, with no conversion at the end.

Take a match (the other side)

An open match on the Orders tab is waiting for someone to take it. Taking it commits you as the opponent. You can never take your own match, and you only ever pay with the same coin the maker used.

Matches with a (Computer backer) tag are filled by the platform's own pool when no human is ready, so a match is never left waiting.

Good to know

Gasless and quiet: posting, taking, locking and settling are all signed for you automatically, so there are no wallet pop-ups and nothing to pay for network fees. Same for the games themselves.

AGM = Automated Game Matcher, the system that opens, takes and settles matches.
P2P = Player vs Player, both seats are real humans.
P2C = Player vs Computer, a computer seat fills the match so no one waits.
Pot = the prize pool: every player's prize level added together.
Open / Locked / Settled = an open match is waiting for a taker; locked means two players are committed; settled means the winner is finalized.
