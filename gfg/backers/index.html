Things people ask before they back

Is this an investment?

No. You are buying a lifetime plan, a badge, and a chance to put the next chapter of the site on firmer ground. Please do not mint because you expect to sell at a profit. Please mint because you want this courtyard to exist on Mainnet.

Why two SOL instead of something smaller?

Because the expensive part is not the picture. It is Mainnet, ongoing sponsored gas so play stays light for everyone, and enough time to build the next native games without the founder running on fumes. A cheaper seat would force us either to inflate the supply or to give up the gasless promise. We would rather tell you the true number.

Why only a hundred?

So this cannot be mistaken for a generic profile-picture farm. A hundred names can still fit on a wall. Ten thousand cannot.

What if only a few people mint?

Then Milestone 1 waits, and we say that out loud. The beta can keep serving Ludo. We will not fake a Mainnet day with money we do not have. That is part of the risk you are being asked to accept, and we know it is a lot to ask.

What if all hundred mint?

Then the LaunchMyNFT page says sold out, we do not add more, and L3 does not get more expensive for the people who already paid.

Do I still pay L1 or L2 every month?

Not on the account that holds the linked pass.

Do I earn the Member Cup automatically?

No. You still join the cup each month, you still need ten thousand raw win points, and then the 1.50× applies. The pass makes you a lifetime member. It does not play the games for you.

Can I play if I never mint?

Yes. Free play is the front door. Monthly plans are the ordinary way into the cup. Early Backer is the lifetime way, and it is optional.

How do I prove I minted?

The LaunchMyNFT transaction and the wallet you connect on GlobalFolkGames. Keep both.
