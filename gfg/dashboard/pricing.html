Ladder rule

Each level is 2x the previous level. L0 is the base FREE plan for new sign-ups. Levels L1 - L10 require premium subscriptions. Features below drive lives, games-per-day, win points, ads and referrals.

Features

F1 - Game Access (G): Free accounts cannot pick their game. The platform rotates one free game per day (all free accounts get the same game, so creating multiple accounts to pick a different game is pointless). Today Ludo free, tomorrow another game, rotating daily across all available games, so a free player can try everything before upgrading (one per day, platform-chosen). L1 and above unlock all games, but a player who plays 4 different games cannot open a 5th that day, even with lives left: remaining lives only work on the same 4 games they already spent at least 1 life on that day. Resets every 24 hours.
F2 - Lives (L): Free-play lives per day per the ladder (5 at L0, 10 at L1, 15 at L2, 20 at L3).
F3 - Win points (100PfeGW): Points earned for every game win: 100 at L0, 150 at L1, 200 at L2, 300 at L3, 400 at L4, 500 at L5, and up per the ladder.
F4 - Ads (FAds / LAds): Full ads on the free plan; Less ads from L1 upward.
F5 - Daily bonus (25PaDB): Daily claimable bonus: 25P at L0, doubling per level (50, 100, 200, 400, 800...).
F6 - Affiliate (20%): 20% of the referred plan's payable USD price, paid ONCE on the referred player's FIRST upgrade only. The referrer does NOT need an active subscription - anyone who referred a player that then upgrades earns the 20%. Later renewals/upgrades by the same referral pay nothing (enforced on-chain, one-time per pair).
