Points Recovery

Diagnose and recover orphaned local points (M3) that never reached the three global ledgers (M4).

How it works: Search a player by email or wallet address. The tool reads their on-chain M3 local points (per game) and the three M4 global ledgers, compares the verified win total, and shows any orphaned points that never reached the global ledgers. One click to sync them. It never lets you add an arbitrary amount or reduce points: the only amount it can credit is the exact gap proven by the player's on-chain M3 ledger, bound to a real win proof.
The three global ledgers (M4):
Pure (unspendable) - only verified local game wins, never diluted by bonuses or multipliers.
Lifetime (unspendable) - every point ever earned from any source (game wins, signup bonus, tier multiplier, referral, etc.).
Spendable - the site-wide balance shown in the header; goes up with earnings and down with spends.
A verified win credits all three at once. This tool only syncs that verified win amount.

When a player contacts support about missing points, ask for:
  • Their email address (the one they sign in with)
  • Or their wallet address (shown on their profile page)
  • Which game they played (Ludo, Ayo Olopon, etc.)
  • When they played (approximate date/time)
  • What position they finished (1st, 2nd, etc.)
The tool will auto-detect orphaned points and offer a one-click sync. It only ever credits the verified amount on the player's on-chain M3 ledger; there is no way to add a custom number or reduce points.
