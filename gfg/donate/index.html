SUPPORT THE PLATFORM

Keep the games free to play

GlobalFolkGames is free, open source, and gasless for players. Nobody pays to play, and nobody signs a wallet popup to make a move. The platform quietly pays the small on-chain costs so the experience stays as simple as a normal web game.

Your support covers the things that are never free: RPC and infrastructure, the one-time on-chain setup for each player, hosting, and the work of adding the next folk games from around the world. Every donation, whatever the size, is genuinely appreciated.

LARGER SUPPORT

Want lifetime access? That is the Early Backer collection

A small or one-time donation of a few dollars is appreciated, but it does not unlock lifetime access. The platform has to stay sustainable for everyone, so lifetime benefits are tied to the limited Early Backer collection, not to donations.

Early Backers get lifetime L3 membership (more daily lives, higher daily rewards, a win multiplier, and no ads) plus a numbered seat. The collection is small on purpose and it is what funds the move toward Mainnet.
