How to get a plan

  1. Tap Pay under your plan. This opens the crypto payment guide with simple, beginner-friendly steps (even if you have never used crypto before).
  2. You pay from your own embedded wallet on the site, so the wallet that pays is the wallet that gets the plan.
  3. The site checks the payment and activates your plan on-chain in one step, usually within seconds.
  4. Your level, lives and rewards start right away. You can follow it on your Upgrade page.
  5. No receipt emails and no waiting: the payment and the activation happen together.

No auto-renew and non-refundable. Nothing charges again unless you choose it. All prices are in US dollars (USDC).

Common questions

Why do higher levels cost more?

Each step up adds bigger rewards: more lives per day, a higher daily reward and win multiplier, and the competition boost (Level 2 = 1.25x, Level 3 = 1.5x). All levels keep ads (you help keep the platform free); higher levels show fewer of them.

Is this the price forever?

The launch beta discount is limited time. The regular price shows struck through on each plan. When the beta ends, the discount is removed. The price you pay at checkout is the price of the plan, and your payment activates it directly.

I have never used crypto. Is this hard?

No. Tap Pay and the guide walks you through it in five simple steps. You top up your embedded wallet once with a little SOL (about $1-2 for future fees), fund it with the exact USDC you need, and pay from the site. The wallet that pays is the wallet that gets the plan.

Can I switch plans?

You run one plan at a time for its 30 days, then choose what to activate next - no auto-renew, so you are never charged on autopilot.
