The fee

A small fee per session, in USDC, charged once at connect: 0.0004 base + 0.0004 per delegated account + 0.0002 per game. Everything inside the session (moves, dice, timers, point credits) is free, so a 200-move game costs the same as a 5-move game. Nothing else is charged.

How to read it

  • Your players pay nothing, always. There is no player-pay option in the rail at all.
  • Batch to drop the per-game cost. One session can cover many matches for the same two delegated accounts; only the per-game part grows, so cost per game keeps falling.
  • Gas shrinks at lower Gwei. At 5 Gwei the Arc gas column is about 4x smaller, and total cost crosses under 1 USDC per 1,000 games around a batch of 4.
  • A real compact game. The numbers are measured on Arc testnet with a real game, not guessed.
